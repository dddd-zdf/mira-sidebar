MIRA SIDEBAR

One shortcut away.

Examples: Control+Space, Control+Shift+Space, Alt+M.
Ctrl+Space can also be used by input language tools.

Normal: no reserved space. Adaptive: reserve after dragging another window to the top to maximize. Always reserve: reserve whenever Mira is visible.

Drag the sidebar's inner edge to resize. Use its menu to dock left or right. Sign in on the real ChatGPT page; Mira never asks for a password.

Inside Mira: Ctrl+T new chat, Ctrl+Shift+N temporary chat, Ctrl+W delete current chat (with confirmation).
